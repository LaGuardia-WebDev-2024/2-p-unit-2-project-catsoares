Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

Reasons that Cat loves Rio..

    The food anywhere is amazing and great quality
    Everything is super cheap
    The people are all so kind and family oriented
    Bossa nova music is great to chill at the beach in the daytime and the funk music is great for dancing in the nighttime
    Weather is always warm and geography is amazing, it's a rare sight! There are mountains right next to the ocean and loads of trees everywhere!
    All of the beaches are so nice and all of them have beautiful sunsets!
    The vibe is the coolest part! I'm Brazilian so my entire family lives there and everytime I'm there I love hearing the Prtuguese!
